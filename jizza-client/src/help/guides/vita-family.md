## The Family account

Jizza has three kinds of account: Individual, Family and Organization. A Family is a small account that only lists its members. Nobody works inside it: everyone keeps their own Individual account, with their own agents and their own Vita.

To create one, open the account menu at the bottom of the left menu and choose **Create a Family**. Give it a name. You become its Owner. A person can own one Family.

Switch to the Family from the account menu to see **Members**, the only page it has. From there:

- **Add member** sends a request by email. Roles are **Admin** (can invite and remove) and **Member**.
- **Remove** takes a person out of the Family. Every share they had ends at once.
- A member can **Leave the Family**. The Owner can't leave.

## Sharing a record

Open [Agents](/agents), then **Vita**, then the **Health** tab, pick the person in **Person**, then open **Sharing**.

The view starts with your Family and its members. **Share with a member** lets you pick a member and what they may see:

- **This is them**: the person's own login. They can add to medicines and vitals and see the rest. Jizza opens on **My medicines today** when they sign in.
- **Emergency only**: the emergency card only.
- **Caregiver**: they can add to medicines and vitals, and see lab results, visits and documents.
- **Everything except journal**: they can see every section except the journal.

**Customise section by section** opens the full list, where each section is **None**, **View** or **Edit**. Giving **Edit** asks for your password again.

Edit means adding entries and correcting their own. Only you, who keep the record, can delete entries or change sharing. Items marked private are never shared.

## The consent note

Before anyone other than the person sees their record, note when the person agreed and how, under **Consent notes**. Linking someone as **This is them** needs no note. Your own record needs none either.

Withdrawing the last open note ends every helper's share of that record.

## The access list

**Access list** shows who can see each section, with the access and the date it started. **Show ended shares** adds the ones that ended and why. **Print** prints the list on its own.

Stopping a share takes effect on the other person's next screen. Pages they already saved or printed can't be recalled.

## A record shared with you

Records shared with you appear in **Person** under **Shared with you**, marked **(shared)**. A banner names who keeps the record and what you may do. You only see the sections shared with you, and the **Add** menu only offers the sections you can edit.

## My medicines today

When you are linked to your own record, **My medicines** appears at the top of the left menu. The page shows today's medicines in large text: press **All taken** for a whole time, or tick one medicine. Each time says who marked it. It also shows your next visit with the doctor's phone, a button to add a reading, your emergency card and **Open my full health record**.

If you mark a dose with no network, it waits and is sent when you are back online.

Jizza opens on this page after you sign in. To turn that off, open Vita's **Settings** and clear **Open Jizza on My medicines after I sign in**.

## Notes between helpers

Once a record is shared, Home has a **Notes** card. You and the people you share with can leave notes there, such as "Ran out of strips, bought a pack". Each note shows who wrote it and when.

## Export or delete a record

Open the person's **Profile**, then **This record**:

- **Export** saves every entry of the record as a file. It works on a closed record too, which helps with insurance claims.
- **Delete** removes every reading, medicine, report, document and note of the person for good, and ends every share. Type the person's name to confirm. The person stays in your list of people.

Both ask for your password again.

## Past records

When a person's record is closed, it moves under **Past records** in **Person**. It says when it closed, can be read and exported, and nothing can be added to it.
