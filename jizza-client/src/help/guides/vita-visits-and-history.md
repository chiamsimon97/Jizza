## Preparing for a visit

Open **Doctors and visits** in Vita's Health tab. An upcoming visit has a **Prepare** button. It opens one page with:

- your questions for the doctor
- readings from the last 30 days, with the target and who set it
- the medicines taken now
- medicine changes since the last visit
- the latest lab results, with the lab's flag
- how many doses were taken

Write questions when you add or change a visit, using **Add a question**. Nothing is sent to the doctor. Press **Print or save as PDF**, or show the page on your phone.

## Health summary

**Health summary**, at the top of Doctors and visits, gives a one-page summary of the whole record to hand to a doctor or a hospital. A condition's page has **Summary** for that condition alone. Medicines marked private are left out unless you tick **Include medicines marked private**.

## Vaccinations

Under **Vaccinations**, **Add a dose** records the vaccine, the dose number, the date given, the place and the batch. Give the date it was given, or the date of the next dose. Upload the certificate in Documents and link it to the dose.

## Surgeries and stays

Under **Surgeries and stays**, add operations, procedures, hospital stays, injuries and falls. Give the start date, and an end date for a stay, along with the hospital and the doctor. A stay shows how many nights it lasted.

## Lab reports from your mailbox

1. Connect Gmail or Outlook under [Connectors](/connectors).
2. In Vita's **Connect**, under **Mailbox**, press **Add a sender**.
3. Name the lab's email address, and the person the reports are for.
4. If the lab locks its PDFs, tick the password box and type the password. Vita keeps it encrypted and never shows it again.

Vita looks only at mail from that sender. **Check now** looks straight away. A report it finds is read and waits in Documents for your review, like an upload. Nothing is saved to the record until you confirm.

Each sender shows when it was last checked and what happened, such as **Nothing new** or **A report is waiting for your review**. **Pause** stops the checks without removing the sender.
