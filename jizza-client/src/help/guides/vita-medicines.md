## Where to find it

Open [Agents](/agents), then **Vita**, then the **Health** tab, then **Medicines**. **Add**, then **Medicine**, at the top of the tab opens the same form.

Vita keeps this list and reminds. It never changes a dose or suggests starting or stopping a medicine. Make changes here only when the doctor has made them.

## Adding a medicine

Give the name, the strength (such as 500 mg) and the times it is taken. Pick **Every day** or the days of the week, how much each time, and whether it is taken before, with or after food.

Tick **Taken only when needed** for a medicine with no fixed times, and pick **Supply** for strips, needles and the like. Neither gets reminders.

**More details** holds the brand and generic names, the system of medicine, the condition it is for, the doctor, the start and end dates and the stock.

## Today's medicines on Home

Home shows one card for each time of day. **All taken** marks every medicine in the card. To mark one alone, tick the box beside it. **Skip** marks the whole card skipped, and **Snooze 30 min** reminds again later.

A marked card says who marked it and when. A dose not marked in time shows as **Missed**; it can still be marked taken or skipped.

## When the doctor changes a dose

Press **Change dose or times** on the medicine. Give the new strength or times, the date it starts and the doctor. The old schedule stays in the history: open the medicine to see its changes and the last two weeks of doses.

**Pause** stops reminders until **Resume**. **Stop** ends the medicine from today and moves it to **Stopped**.

## Stock and refills

Turn on **Track stock** and give the units in hand. Each dose marked taken counts down, and Vita reminds before it runs out by the number of days you choose. Press **Refill** when you buy more; the pack size is filled in for you.

The list shows how much of each medicine was taken in the last 30 days, counted from the doses that were due.
