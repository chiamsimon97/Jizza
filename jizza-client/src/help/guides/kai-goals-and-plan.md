## Goals

Open **Kai**, then **Portfolio**, then **Goals**. Each goal shows its target date, the amount grown by inflation, how funded it is at today's pace, and the monthly amount that would reach it.

Press **New goal** to pick from the library: emergency fund, retirement, child education, home down payment, car, travel, wedding or your own. Kai fills in the amount, the date and the priority from what you told it in Quick start. You only correct what is wrong. The date decides the bucket: **Short** is under 3 years, **Mid** is 3 to 7 years, **Long** is 7 years or more.

Steps 5, 7 and 8 of the wizard (starting mix, glide path, rules) open once you add holdings.

## Does a goal meet its target?

Kai checks every goal against its target and gives it a verdict: **On track**, **At risk**, **Off track** or **Not enough data**.

Markets don't grow by the same amount every year. So Kai plays out thousands of possible markets, some good and some poor, and counts how often each goal gets there. That count is the goal's **chance**. Each priority has a bar: 90% for survival goals, 75% for important ones and 50% for aspirational ones. You can change the bars in **Settings**.

- **On track**: the chance is at or above the bar, and the goal's mix is inside its bands.
- **At risk**: the chance is up to 15 points below the bar, or the mix has drifted outside a band.
- **Off track**: the chance is further below the bar, or nothing is set aside for the goal yet.

Each goal card shows its chance against the bar. Open a goal to see what you'd have in a poor market (1 in 10) and in a typical one, and how much more a month would close any gap. When you've added holdings and said which goal each unit is for, the check uses what those units are worth.

Kai runs the check every night and sends you a notification when a verdict changes. The notification names the goal, never an amount. Press **Check again** on the Goals screen to run it now. Kai only tells you whether a goal is on course. It never buys or sells for you.

Open a goal to see the funding sum, the mix its bucket starts from, and when it moves to the next bucket. You can edit it, pause it, archive it or mark it achieved.

Until you add holdings, Kai assumes its default yearly returns for each bucket. They are listed under **Settings**.

## Plan builder

Enter what you can invest each month and, if you like, a one-time lumpsum. Kai then:

1. Takes survival needs **off the top** first: an emergency buffer and loans costing more than 12% a year.
2. Gives each goal the monthly amount it needs, in its own bucket.
3. Splits the rest into Short, Mid and Long by your age, then into asset classes and sub-categories by your risk profile.

Every line says where its figure comes from: Kai's table (with the age band or risk profile), a goal, or **You**. Press any amount to change it. The lines beside it rebalance so the total stays what you entered. **Reset to Kai default** undoes your changes.

A lumpsum goes into Short, Mid and Long debt and gold today. The Long equity part is parked in a liquid fund and moved in over 6 months.

**Save plan** keeps the split. Nothing is bought. Kai names sub-categories such as "Flexi cap", never a particular fund.

## Tax

**Tax** compares the new and old regimes for one person and the current financial year (April to March). Kai needs gross pay, from Quick start's income question. Enter deductions such as 80C and 80D, and rent if you claim HRA, then press **Compare again**.

A yellow banner means some tax figures in **Settings** have not been verified for this year. Check them with your CA before you act. Capital gains and advance-tax dates come with holdings.

## Risk profile

Quick start's last question asks six things about how you react to losses. Kai also looks at what your finances can carry: steady income, dependants, emergency money, debt and how soon your nearest goal is. Your profile is the more careful of the two. It is indicative only, not the suitability check a SEBI-registered adviser must do.
