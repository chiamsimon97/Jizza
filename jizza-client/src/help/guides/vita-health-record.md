## Where to find it

Open [Agents](/agents), then **Vita**, then the **Health** tab. The tab shows only while health is switched on for your Jizza.

Vita keeps a health record for you and for the people you look after who have no login of their own here, such as a parent or a child. Kai sees the same list of people, so a person added in one place appears in the other.

Vita is not a doctor. It never names a diagnosis and never suggests starting, stopping or changing a medicine.

## Choosing a person

The **Person** box at the top switches between records. **+ Add a person…** adds someone you look after and opens Quick start for them. A person whose record is closed appears under **Past records** and can only be read.

## Quick start

Eight short steps, one per screen: date of birth, sex at birth, height and weight, blood group, known conditions, allergies, current medicines, and one emergency contact. Each step is saved as soon as you press **Next**, so you can stop with **Finish later** and carry on from Home. **Skip** leaves a step for later. Answering a step again replaces what that step added the first time.

Medicines arrive with the next update, so for now the medicines step only takes **Later**.

## The emergency card

**Emergency card** shows what a stranger or a hospital needs in the first minutes: name, age, blood group, allergies (most severe first, or "None known"), conditions, emergency contacts, the preferred hospital with its phone, organ donor, and the emergency number.

- **Print or save as PDF** prints the card alone, on one page.
- **Save image for lock screen** makes a tall picture of the card for a phone's lock screen. Anyone holding the phone can read it.
- Add up to five emergency contacts below the card.
- A condition marked **Keep it off the emergency card** stays in the record but not on the card.

Edit the details in **Profile**, opened from the card or from Settings.

## Conditions and allergies

Add a condition from the common list or type your own. Type 2 diabetes and high blood pressure get pages of their own with the next update. When a person has none, press **Mark "None known"**: that is a recorded answer, not an empty list, and it shows on the card.

Allergies work the same way, with the kind, the reaction, how severe it is and whether a doctor confirmed it.

## Weight

Add a weight from **Add** or from **Latest readings** on Home. Blood pressure, sugar and pulse come with the next update. Every chart can be shown as a table.

## Settings and the figures Vita uses

Settings holds your units (weight in kg or lb, height in cm or feet and inches; values are always stored in metric), the emergency number, and the ambulance number where the person lives.

Every clinical figure carries one of three labels:

- **Vita default** with a **Needs verification** badge: taken from a named source, but nobody has checked it against that source yet.
- **from Dr …**: a target a doctor set for that person (added with the doctors update). It always replaces Vita's default.
- **Not set: ask your doctor**: no source was found, so Vita has no number and works nothing out from it.

## Telling Vita in chat

You can tell Vita something in plain words, such as "my blood group is O+". Vita puts a short form in the chat with what it understood. Nothing is saved until you check it and press **Save**. What you type in the shared chat stays in that chat's history, which later replies by other agents can see.

## Red flags

From the readings update on, a reading at a level where doctors advise urgent care shows a banner first. **Act today** gives the advice and asks whether any warning signs are there now. **Call now** shows the emergency number, the person's preferred hospital and a link to their emergency card. The banner stays until someone acknowledges it.
