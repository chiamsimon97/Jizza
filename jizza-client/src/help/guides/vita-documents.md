## Where to find it

Open [Agents](/agents), then **Vita**, then the **Health** tab, then **Documents**. **Add**, then **Document or report**, at the top of the tab opens the upload.

Documents are kept encrypted. Tick **Private** to keep one to the record's manager only.

## Uploading

Drop a PDF or a photo on the upload area, or press **Choose a file**. PDFs can be up to 10 MB and photos up to 5 MB. Pick the kind if you know it, or leave **Let Vita tell**, then press **Upload and read**.

Vita reads the document and opens the review. Nothing is saved to the record until you confirm.

## Checking what Vita read

The review shows the document on one side and what Vita read on the other. Every row can be changed.

- **Outlined cells** are ones Vita is unsure of. Compare them with the document.
- **Test results** show the lab's own flag (such as High) as printed. Whether a result is within your target is shown separately once it is saved.
- **Medicines** show what was written, such as 1-0-1 after food, beside the times Vita took from it. Check the times and days.
- When Vita **could not read a medicine's name**, the name is left empty. Type it from the prescription, or tick **Leave out**. Vita never guesses a name.
- Tick **Leave out** on any row you do not want saved.

Press **Confirm** to save the rows you kept. Medicines are added to Medicines with the times you checked, and reminders start from today. **Discard what Vita read** throws the reading away and keeps the document.

## When the patient may be someone else

If the name, age or sex on the document does not fit the record, the review says so and holds **Confirm**. Either tick **I checked: it belongs to this person**, or press **Move it to the right person**.

## Locked PDFs

Labs often lock reports with a password, such as the date of birth. Type it and press **Unlock and read**. Vita keeps the password encrypted so it can read the next report from the same lab.

## Finding a document later

Search the titles, or filter by kind, doctor, condition or year. The pencil edits a document's details, the arrows move it to another person, and **What was saved** shows what was confirmed from it.
