## Where to find it

Open [Agents](/agents), then **Kai**, then the **Portfolio** tab. The tab shows only while finance is switched on for your Jizza.

Kai looks at your own money and the money of people you manage who have no login, such as a child or a parent. Kai suggests; you decide. In this release Kai never buys, sells or moves anything.

## Quick start

Six questions, one per screen, take about 10 minutes:

1. Whose money you look after here
2. What comes in each month (take-home, and gross if you know it)
3. What goes out: needs, everything else, and what goes into savings or SIPs
4. Loans and borrowings, with the rate the way you know it ("14% a year", "₹2 per ₹100 a month", "no interest")
5. Term and health cover, ticking **Only through my employer** where that applies
6. Rough totals of what you have saved

Each answer is saved as you press **Next**, so you can stop with **Finish later** and carry on where you left off. **Skip for now** leaves that question's checks as **Not enough data**. A skipped question never counts against you. Answering a question again replaces what you said before.

At the end you see your score, net worth, what is left each month, and your next three steps.

Want to look around first? On the first Home screen press **Look around with sample data**. It shows a made-up household and saves nothing. Press **Leave sample** to go back.

## The Money Health score

The score is out of 100 and comes from six checks:

| Check | What it measures |
| --- | --- |
| Emergency fund | Months of essential spending your emergency money covers |
| Debt | Loan payments as a share of income, and any loan above the high-interest line |
| Savings rate | Money that moves into savings or SIPs each month, as a share of take-home |
| Goal funding | How well important goals are funded (comes with goals in the next release) |
| Protection | Term and health cover against what each person needs |
| Spending | Needs as a share of take-home |

Green earns a check's full points, amber half, red none. A grey check has no data yet: it is left out and the score is worked out over the rest. The targets are Kai's defaults; you can change them in **Settings**.

Two things are flagged but not scored: a missing nominee and no will.

## The action plan

**Actions** lists what Kai suggests, grouped as Survival, Important, Aspirational and Hygiene. Three are open at a time, most urgent first; the rest wait in **Later**. While a Survival check is red, Important actions wait.

For each action press **Done**, **Snooze 30 days**, or **Not for me** with a short reason. A dismissed action does not come back.

## Cash flow, loans, protection and net worth

- **Cash flow** shows take-home by person, needs, wants and saved against the 50/30/20 guide, and where Kai suggests the surplus goes. Loan payments come from your loans, so they are never counted twice.
- **Loans** shows each loan with its real yearly rate and what you typed, gold loans with their loan-to-value against the cap, and a repayment plan. Switch between **Highest rate first** and **Smallest balance first**, and move **Extra each month** to see the debt-free date change. A loan from family or a friend stays private: anyone you share with sees "Family / friend".
- **Protection** shows each person's term cover against need, with how the need is worked out, and health cover against the personal floor. Employer cover is shown but does not count, because it ends with the job. Kai never names an insurer.
- **Net worth** shows what you own and owe, by type and by person, with the date each value was set. Kai saves a snapshot on the 1st of each month.

Figures marked **Needs verification** use a tax or regulatory number nobody has confirmed for this year. Check them with your CA before relying on them.

## Ask Kai

Press **Ask Kai** in the Portfolio bar. Chat opens with a question about the screen you were on already started; finish it and send. Kai reads your figures without asking first and quotes Kai's own calculations.

## Alerts

Kai sends a notification for policy renewals in the next 30 days, loans due in the next 60, a gold loan close to its cap, a child turning 18 in the next 90 days, an emergency fund under three months, and figures that need verification. The same alert is not repeated within 30 days.
