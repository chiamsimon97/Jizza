## Where to find it

Open [Agents](/agents), then **Vita**, then the **Health** tab, then **Lab results**. **Upload a report** opens the upload in Documents.

A report reaches Lab results only after you confirm what Vita read from it. Until then it waits in Documents for your review.

## Reading a report

A report lists its tests in groups: Diabetes, Kidney, Lipids, Liver, Thyroid, Blood count, Vitamins and Other. Each test shows:

- **The result.** When the lab printed another unit, Vita shows the result in the usual unit and says what was printed, such as "Printed 3.1 mmol/L, shown in mg/dL". Until the conversion is checked, it carries **Needs verification**.
- **The lab flag**, such as "Lab: high (4.0–5.6)". This is the lab's own judgement from its printed range.
- **Your target**, with who set it: the doctor, your own range, or a Vita default marked **Needs verification**. A test with no target reads "Not set: ask your doctor".

The lab flag and your target are two separate things. A result can be high for the lab and still within the target your doctor set.

For HbA1c, Vita also shows the value in mmol/mol and the estimated average glucose. These never replace the measured value.

## Following a test over time

Press **Trend** beside a test to see it across reports. It shows the change since the first report and since the one before. **Show as a table** lists each report's value, lab flag and target status.

## Correcting or deleting a report

**Correct** lets you change any row as the lab printed it, or remove a row. Vita works out the unit, the lab flag and the target again when you save. The bin deletes the whole report. The document stays in Documents.

## Lab targets

**Lab targets** on the Lab results page lists HbA1c and any test someone set a range for. **Add doctor's target** records the doctor's figure with the date they set it. **Set own range** records your own. The doctor's target always comes first.

## Checks

Record eye, foot, dental and other checks under **Doctors and visits**, then **Checks**, or on a condition's page. Give the date the check was done, or the date the doctor gave for the next one.

A condition's page shows each check as **Due**, **Coming up**, **No record yet** or **Not set: ask your doctor**, with where the date comes from:

- the doctor's date, or
- the interval in Settings, which is a Vita default marked **Needs verification** until it is checked.

Vita works out no interval of its own.
