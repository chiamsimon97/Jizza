## Open the setup guide

Open **Agents**, then **Kai**, then **Portfolio**, and press **Setup guide** at the top right. The guide lists every step in five stages. It shows what each step is for, what to have to hand and about how long it takes.

Press **Go to** on a step to open its screen. A bar along the bottom stays with you while you work. It shows which step you are on, with **All steps** to go back and **Next** to move on. Close the bar with **×** when you want to look around on your own.

Steps that Kai can see tick themselves, such as a saved goal or an imported file. For the others, press **Mark as done**. Optional steps can be skipped, and you can undo a skip later.

Home shows a **Set up your Portfolio** card with your progress and the next step until everything is done. Close it with **×**, or press **Show the guide on Home** in the guide to bring it back.

## Stage 1: Start with the basics

1. **Answer Quick start.** There are eight short questions: income, spending, loans, cover, savings, goals, the people you plan for and your attitude to risk. You get your first Money Health score and the three things to do first. Have your take-home pay, loan balances and policy cover amounts to hand. You can skip any question.
2. **Add the people you manage** (optional). Add a spouse, child or parent whose money you look after. Each person has their own cover, tax and goals.
3. **Look over Kai's defaults** (optional). Kai plans with figures for inflation, expected returns, emergency months and tax. Change any that don't fit you. Check any figure marked "needs verification".

## Stage 2: Set goals and a plan

4. **Set your goals.** For each goal, enter what it would cost today and when you need it. Kai adds inflation, works out the monthly amount to put in and suggests a starting mix.
5. **Build your plan.** Enter what you can invest each month. Kai splits it across short, mid and long term, down to each kind of fund. Press **Save plan**.

## Stage 3: Bring your money in

6. **Bring in bank statements.** Under **Connect**, import a CSV or PDF statement for one to three months. Check every row before you save. You can undo an import later.
7. **Bring in your investments.** Import your mutual fund statement (CAS) from CAMS or KFintech with its password. Also import your broker's holdings file or tradebook. Kai builds your holdings with each purchase kept as a tax lot.

## Stage 4: Put investments to work

8. **Give each unit a goal.** Open **Investments**, then **Holdings**, and open each holding. Type how many units belong to each goal; the total must match the units held. The step ticks itself once no holding has units left in **Unassigned**. Moving units between goals is not a sale, so no tax arises.
9. **Check each goal's mix** (optional). Under **Asset tree**, pick a goal and save Kai's starting mix as it is, or change the targets. Each group must add up to 100%.

## Stage 5: Protect and tidy up

10. **Record your cover.** Under **Protection**, add each term and health policy with its cover, premium and renewal date. Kai compares cover with what each person needs and reminds you before a renewal.
11. **Compare tax regimes** (optional). Under **Tax**, enter your deductions. Kai works out both regimes for the year so you can choose.

## When you are done

The guide says your Portfolio is set up once every step is done or skipped. After that, Kai keeps your score, goals and holdings up to date each night. Import new statements whenever you like. Kai only watches and suggests; it never buys, sells or moves money.
