## Where to find it

Open [Agents](/agents), then **Vita**, then the **Health** tab, then **Vitals**. The **Add** button at the top also adds a reading for the person picked in the **Person** box.

Vita is not a doctor. It shows your readings against a target and says who set that target. It never names a diagnosis and never suggests starting, stopping or changing a medicine.

## Adding a reading

Pick the kind, type the numbers and press **Save reading**. Leave **When** empty for now.

- **Blood pressure**: the top number, the bottom number, the pulse if you have it, and whether you took it at home or at the clinic.
- **Sugar**: the number in mg/dL and when you took it: fasting, before a meal, after a meal, at bedtime or any time.
- **Weight** and **temperature** use the units you chose in **Settings**.

After a blood pressure reading, **Add a second reading** a minute or two later. Vita keeps both and shows the average.

If the same numbers were saved a moment ago, Vita asks **Already saved. Add anyway?** so a reading is not counted twice.

A reading typed for the wrong person can be moved with the arrows button beside it. The pencil corrects it and the bin deletes it.

## Targets and where they come from

Every reading shows **In range**, **Outside range**, **Red flag** or **Target not set**, in words as well as colour. Beside it Vita says which target it used:

- **from Dr …, set on …**: a target a doctor set for this person. This always comes first.
- **Your own range**: a range you set yourself.
- **Vita default**: Vita's general figure. Most carry a **Needs verification** badge until someone checks them against their source.
- **Not set: ask your doctor**: Vita has no figure to use.

Add a doctor's target under **Targets** on the Vitals page. Weight has no target in Vita.

## When a reading is a red flag

If a reading you typed is at a red-flag level, Vita first asks **Is 182/112 right?** Nobody is alerted until you say yes. If you typed it wrong, press **No**, then correct or delete it.

After a yes, Vita shows help at one of two levels, in the words set in **Settings**:

- **Act today**: call the doctor today. If one of the listed signs is happening now, press it and then **Yes, one of these now**.
- **Call now**: call the emergency number shown, and open the emergency card.

The person who manages the record is alerted once. The alert carries no numbers and no diagnosis. Banners stay on Home for a day after the reading, unless Settings says otherwise.

## Doctors and visits

**Doctors and visits** lists the household's doctors with their phone, the next visit and the targets each doctor set. Visits for the person picked show upcoming first, then past visits with notes and follow-up dates.

## Condition pages

Open a condition from **Conditions**. Diabetes and high blood pressure pages show the key readings with their averages, the chart for the last week or month, and the checks that are due, or **Not set: ask your doctor** where Vita has no figure.
