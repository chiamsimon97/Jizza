## Connect a phone

Connections are made in the Jizza app on the phone: **Health Connect** on Android, **Apple Health** on an iPhone. Pick the person, then turn on only the data you want to send: steps, sleep, workouts, resting heart rate, blood pressure, sugar, weight, oxygen or temperature. The app only reads; it never writes to Health Connect or Apple Health.

## See what arrived

Open [Agents](/agents), then **Vita**, then the **Health** tab, pick the person, then **Activity and sleep**. It shows steps for each of the last 14 days, sleep with bedtime and wake time, recent workouts and resting heart rate, each with the phone it came from. Blood pressure, sugar and weight from a phone appear under **Vitals** with the other readings, and a reading the phone also sent is kept once.

## Stop a phone, or delete what it added

Open **Connect** and find **Phones and watches**. Each connection shows its phone, when it last synced and how many readings each data type added.

- **Turn off** stops one data type; the rest keep coming.
- **Remove** stops the connection; nothing more is accepted through it.

Either way, Vita then asks **Delete what it added?** Deleting is a separate choice and cannot be undone. **Keep them** leaves the readings in the record; a stopped connection keeps the **Delete what it added** button until you use it.
