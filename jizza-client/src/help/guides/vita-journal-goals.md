## Where to find them

Open [Agents](/agents), then **Vita**, then the **Health** tab, pick the person in **Person**, then open **Journal** or **Goals**.

## The daily journal

1. Pick a face for **Mood** and one for **Energy**. Press a face again to clear it.
2. Slide **Pain** from 0 (none) to 10 (worst). Leave it alone if there is no pain to note.
3. Tap the **symptoms** that fit, or type another and press **Add**.
4. Add a **note** if you like, then press **Save**. Nothing is kept until you press it.

The month on the right marks each day with an entry. Pick a day to read it or change it. A day still to come cannot be filled in.

The journal is private. Nobody in your Family reads it unless you share the **Journal** section with them under **Sharing**.

## Goals

1. Press **Add a goal** and pick the kind.
2. For **weight, steps, sleep or water**, type the number you are aiming for. For a weight goal, Vita uses the latest weight on record as the start unless you type one.
3. For **blood pressure, sugar or HbA1c**, you type no number. The goal reads the target your doctor set, or Vita's default with a **Needs verification** badge and "Confirm with your doctor". If neither is set, the goal cannot be added yet: ask the doctor for a target and add it to the doctor's targets first.
4. Type the **weekly plan**, one step a line. Vita does not suggest plan figures.
5. Press **Save goal**.

Each week fills in from the readings already in the record where it can. Press **Check in** to type a week's value yourself. The marks on each goal show the last 12 weeks: a tick when the week met the goal, a cross when it did not, an empty circle when there was no value. Each mark says this in words when you point at it. A goal is marked **Met** after it is met for the number of weeks in a row shown on its card (4 unless changed in Settings).

You can **Pause**, **Resume**, **Mark met** or **Stop** a goal. Met and stopped goals sit under **Met and stopped**, where you can start one again.

## The weekly note

Open **Weekly note** in the Health tab. Every Sunday evening Vita writes what changed in the record that week: medicine changes, missed doses, readings outside the range, lab reports added, visits coming up and refills due. Each sentence is built from entries in the record, and the links under it open them. Use the arrows to read an earlier week. The note goes to whoever keeps the record and, when they have their own login, to the person themself, and the inbox item opens it.

## Goes with

Under the journal, **Goes with** looks for what goes together: mood, energy or pain beside sleep, steps, missed doses or readings outside range. It stays silent until the journal has enough days (21 unless changed), and it only says something when the link is clear. Press **Show the days** to see every day behind a statement. Going together does not mean one causes the other.

Both carry "Prepared by Vita from your own records. Not medical advice."
