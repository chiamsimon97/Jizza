## Where to find it

Open [Agents](/agents), then **Vita**, then the **Health** tab, pick the person in **Person**, then open **Insurance**. It lists the policies that cover that person and their claims.

## Adding a policy

Press **Add a policy** and fill in:

- **Kind**: health insurance, super top-up, critical illness, personal accident, or a government scheme such as PM-JAY, CGHS, ECHS or ESIC.
- **Insurer** or **Scheme**, and the **Sum insured**. A government scheme can go without one.
- **Renews on**: this sets the policy year and a renewal reminder.
- **Who it covers**: tick everyone on the policy. A family floater is one policy covering several people, so add it once.
- **Provided by an employer** for group cover from work.

**Card and policy details** holds the policy number, the card number, the TPA, the cashless card (a document you uploaded), the room rent limit, the co-pay and the waiting periods. The policy and card numbers are shown only as their last four digits once saved.

## Used and left this policy year

Each policy shows its policy year and two figures:

- **Used this policy year**: what settled claims were paid in the current policy year.
- **Left this policy year**: the sum insured minus what was used.

A stay after the renewal date counts in the next policy year.

## Claims

Press **Add a claim** after a hospital stay. Pick the policy, link the stay from **Surgeries and stays** if you added it, and enter the dates and the amount claimed. When the claim is **Settled**, add the amount paid; Vita shows what you paid yourself as **Out of pocket**.

Status reads Draft, Filed, Insurer asked a question, Settled or Rejected.

## Shared records and Kai

Someone you share the insurance section with sees the policies covering that person, without the used and left figures, and can't change policies.

Kai's **Protection** view reads these policies, so you only keep them in one place. Vita never names, compares or recommends an insurer or a scheme.
